# Getting started with hardware firewalls
{: #getting-started}

Effective 17 December 2025, the {{site.data.keyword.cloud}} shared hardware firewall reached End of Marketing (EOM) and no longer accepts new orders. Additionally, this service will reach End of Support (EOS) on 31 December 2026. After this date, it will no longer be supported or available for use on {{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}}. For migration guidance, see [Migrate off a shared hardware firewall](/docs/hardware-firewall-shared?topic=hardware-firewall-shared-shared-fw-migration).
{: deprecated}

The hardware firewall provides an essential layer of security that is provisioned on demand without service interruptions. It prevents unwanted traffic from reaching your servers, reducing your attack surface, and allowing your server resources to be dedicated for their intended use.
{: shortdesc}

To add a firewall to a server, complete the following steps:

1. In your browser, open the [{{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} catalog](/catalog){: external} and log in to your account.
1. Select the **Navigation Menu** icon ![Menu icon](../../icons/icon_hamburger.svg), then click **Infrastructure > Classic Infrastructure**.
1. From the devices list, click the server that you want to protect.
1. On the **Device Details** page, in the **Configuration** tab, scroll to the **Add-ons** section and click **Order Hardware Firewall**.
1. In the **Order Hardware Firewall** dialog, select from the options presented (if applicable) and click **Continue** to complete the ordering process. The firewall speed defaults to the speed of the public network interface of your server.
