# Bypassing the hardware firewall rules
{: #bypassing-the-hardware-firewall-shared-rules}

You can bypass your hardware firewall rules by completing the following steps.
{: shortdesc}

1. In your browser, open the [{{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} catalog](/catalog){: external} and log in to your account.
1. Select the **Navigation Menu** icon ![Menu icon](../../icons/icon_hamburger.svg), then click **Infrastructure > Classic Infrastructure**.
1. From the devices list, click the firewall-protected device that you want to bypass.
1. On the **Firewall** tab, click the **Actions** menu and select **Bypass Rules**. Click **Yes** to confirm the action. Bypassing rules takes approximately two minutes to take effect. While in bypass mode, the status displays **Bypassing All Rules**.

## Enabling the rules again
{: #enable-the-rules-again}

To enable the rules again, return to the **Firewall** tab of the device, click the **Actions** menu, and select **Process Rules**. The status changes back to **Processing All Rules** within two minutes.
