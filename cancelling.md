# Canceling a hardware firewall
{: #canceling-a-hardware-firewall-shared}
{: help}
{: support}

You can cancel your hardware firewall at any time by completing the following steps.
{: shortdesc}

1. In your browser, open the [{{site.data.keyword.cloud_notm}} catalog](/catalog){: external} and log in to your account.
1. Select the **Navigation Menu** icon ![Menu icon](../../icons/icon_hamburger.svg), then click **Infrastructure > Classic Infrastructure**.
1. From the devices list, click the firewall-protected device that you want to cancel.
1. In the **Add-ons** section, click **Firewall details**. This opens the **Firewall Details** page.
1. On the **Firewall Details** page, click the **Actions** menu and select **Cancel firewall**. In the next dialog, select whether you want to cancel immediately or at the next billing cycle.

All devices on the VLAN are left without front-end firewall protections. This service is provided month to month and billing will be discontinued for the next month upon cancellation.
